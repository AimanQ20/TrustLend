Lender: fund an approved loan

Requires an active LENDER. The amount is read from the workflow and must match exactly. Funds stay in escrow until the borrower accepts or a dispute is resolved.

Borrower: accept funds / repay

Requires the loan's own active BORROWER. Repayment must equal the loan amount (no interest in this version).

Dispute

Only while the deposit is still held in escrow. Freezes the money until an arbiter decides.

Withdraw

Pull payment: money is credited first, then you withdraw it.
